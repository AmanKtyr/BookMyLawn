Our Genesis & Journey

Reimagining Indian Wedding Venue Planning

Bridging the gap between discerning families and authentic celebration spaces.

In India, a wedding is not just an event — it is a momentous milestone uniting families, heritage, and joy.

Yet, for generations, finding the perfect wedding lawn or banquet hall was burdened with opacity: commission-driven brokers, inflated quotes, inaccurate guest capacity claims, and exhausting physical visits across the city.

BookMyLawn was created to change this narrative. We bring modern technology, verified venue audits, and direct communication to the heart of the Indian wedding industry. By auditing venue capacity, catering standards, and authentic per-plate pricing, we give couples the clarity and confidence they deserve.
