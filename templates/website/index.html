Key Factors to Consider When Selecting a Wedding Lawn

When choosing a marriage lawn or party plot, consider:

  • Guest Capacity: Ensure the lawn accommodates your floating guest count comfortably (typically 500 to 2,000+ guests for Indian weddings) without overcrowding the dining area or mandap.
  • Lawn + Banquet Hall Combo: Venues offering both outdoor manicured lawns and indoor AC banquet halls provide flexibility for sudden weather changes, sangeet performances, and dining setups.
  • Catering Policies & Price Per Plate: Inquire whether the venue mandates in-house catering or permits outside wedding caterers. Review veg and non-veg plate costs, live chaat stalls, and dessert counters.
  • Parking & Guest Accommodation: Check for valet parking facilities, dedicated bridal dressing suites, and guest rooms for outstation family members.
