Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use BookMyLawn to search for wedding lawns, banquet halls, or wedding vendors, we collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Wedding Details: Tentative wedding dates, preferred event cities, estimated guest count, and budget.
  • Vendor Inquiries: Messages, dates, and contact requests sent to venue managers or wedding vendors.
  • Reviews & Ratings: Reviews and ratings you submit for vendors you have booked or contacted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Connect you with wedding venues and vendors you specifically inquire about.
  • Facilitate venue availability checks, price estimates, and bookings.
  • Maintain account security and prevent fraudulent inquiries.
  • Improve our search algorithms, website speed, and recommendations.

3. Sharing of Information

We never sell your personal information. When you submit an inquiry for a venue or vendor, we share your name, phone number, and event details with that specific vendor so they can provide availability and quotes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and restricted database access to safeguard your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy officer at privacy@bookmylawn.com.
