Wedding venue pricing typically follows one of two models: (1) Per-Plate Catering Package: The venue rental is included in the food cost, starting from ₹1,000 to ₹3,500+ per plate for vegetarian and ₹1,400 to ₹4,500+ for non-vegetarian menus. (2) Pure Ground Rental: A flat fee (₹1,00,000 to ₹10,00,000+ per day) where you pay separately for space and hire outside caterers.

This varies by venue. Many standalone marriage gardens and farmhouses allow outside wedding caterers upon payment of a kitchen royalty fee. In contrast, 5-star hotels and luxury banquet halls usually mandate using their in-house culinary teams. Each venue profile on BookMyLawn specifies whether outside catering is permitted.

Venues holding a commercial bar license can serve alcohol directly. If you wish to bring your own liquor, you must obtain a temporary one-day excise permit (e.g. P-10 permit in UP/Delhi) through the venue or local excise portal. Corkage charges may apply depending on the property's house policy.

The starting package prices displayed on BookMyLawn represent base rates. In India, luxury event hospitality incurs 18% GST on venue space rental and 5% to 18% GST on catering services. We recommend confirming whether quotes are GST-inclusive prior to signing the booking agreement.

Under environmental noise regulations in India, outdoor sound systems and DJ music in open-air wedding lawns must shut down by 10:00 PM or 10:30 PM (or shift to low-volume ambient music). Indoor sound-insulated banquet halls are typically permitted to continue music and celebrations later into the night subject to local city permissions.

Most wedding lawns partner with 2 to 4 empanelled wedding decorators specializing in floral mandaps, fairy light canopies, and stage backdrops. Some venues permit outside decorators subject to a refundable security deposit or decoration royalty fee.

Premier wedding lawns provide 100% DG (Diesel Generator) power backup to prevent blackouts during heavy lighting, sound systems, and AC operations. Most lawns also feature dedicated parking accommodating 100 to 500+ vehicles, often with complimentary valet service.

The standard payment milestone schedule in India is: (1) Booking Token: 25% to 30% advance upon contract signing. (2) Second Installment: 40% to 50% paid 30 to 45 days prior to the wedding date. (3) Final Balance: Remaining 20% to 25% cleared 2 to 7 days before the event or on the wedding morning.

If you notify the venue 60 to 90 days in advance, most venue owners allow date rescheduling within the same calendar year without forfeiting your advance payment (subject to date availability). In case of total cancellation, advance tokens are usually non-refundable unless stated otherwise in the agreement.
